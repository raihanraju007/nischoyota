## How do I place an order?

Browse or search for a product, open its page, choose any options (like size or colour), then tap **Add to Cart**. When you're ready, go to your cart and tap **Proceed to Checkout**, fill in your delivery details, choose Cash on Delivery and place your order.

## What is Cash on Delivery?

Cash on Delivery (COD) means you pay in cash to the delivery person when your order arrives at your door. You don't need a card or mobile wallet — it's the easiest and safest way to shop online.

## How long does delivery take?

Inside Dhaka, most orders arrive within 1–2 business days. Outside Dhaka, delivery usually takes 2–5 business days depending on your location.

## Can I return a product?

Yes. We offer a 7-day easy return on eligible products, as long as the item is unused and in its original packaging with the invoice. See our [Return & Refund Policy](/return-policy) for details.

## How do I track my order?

You can track your order any time from the [Track Order](/track-order) page using your order number and phone number, or from [My Orders](/account/orders) if you have an account.

## Do I need an account to order?

No — you can check out as a guest. However, creating a free account lets you track orders, save addresses and manage your wishlist more easily.
