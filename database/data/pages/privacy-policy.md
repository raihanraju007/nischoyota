## Information We Collect

When you shop with us we may collect your name, phone number, email address and delivery address. If you place an order we also keep a record of the products you bought and your order history. We do not collect or store any card or banking details, as our only payment method is Cash on Delivery.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process and deliver your orders, contact you about your order status, provide customer support, and — if you opt in — send you occasional offers. We never sell your personal information to anyone.

## Sharing of Information

We share your name, address and phone number with our delivery partners solely so they can deliver your order. We do not share your information with any other third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Access to customer data is limited to staff who need it to serve you.

## Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember your cart, keep you logged in and improve your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as well.

## Your Rights

You may request to view, correct or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us. You may also unsubscribe from promotional messages whenever you wish.
