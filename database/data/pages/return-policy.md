## 7-Day Easy Returns

You can return most products within 7 days of delivery. To be eligible, the item must be unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the invoice.

## How to Request a Return

1. Contact our support team by phone or email with your order number.
2. We arrange a courier pickup or you drop the item at a nearby point.
3. Our team inspects the returned item.
4. Once approved, we process your refund or replacement.

## Non-returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, some items cannot be returned — including innerwear, grooming products that have been opened, and electrical items that have been installed or used, unless they are defective.

## Refunds

For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are issued through mobile banking (bKash/Nagad) within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

## Damaged or Wrong Product

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund at no cost to you.
