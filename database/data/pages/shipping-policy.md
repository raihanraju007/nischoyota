## Delivery Coverage

We deliver to all 64 districts of Bangladesh through our trusted courier partners. If your area is very remote, our team will call to confirm availability before dispatch.

## Delivery Time

- **Inside Dhaka:** 1–2 business days
- **Outside Dhaka:** 2–5 business days

## Delivery Charges

- **Inside Dhaka:** ৳60
- **Outside Dhaka:** ৳120
- **FREE delivery** on all orders over ৳2,000

## Order Processing

After you place an order we confirm it by phone, then pack and hand it to the courier — usually within 24 hours. You'll be able to follow progress on the [Track Order](/track-order) page.

## Cash on Delivery

You pay in cash when your order arrives. You are welcome to inspect the outside of the package and check the item at the door before making payment.

## Delays & Issues

Occasionally, weather, holidays or courier issues may cause delays. If your order is taking longer than expected, please contact us and we'll look into it right away.
